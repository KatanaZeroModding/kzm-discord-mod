# Third-party materials

Room names, sound ids, and object names are read from the player's own game at runtime.

- **Portraits** in `assets/avatars/` are head crops of Katana ZERO idle sprites: `spr_player_nosword_casual_idle` (Zero, standing at home), `spr_dragon_idle` (Fifteen), and `spr_snow_idle` (Snow). The art belongs to Askiisoft. The crops exist so the Discord application can use the same keys as the in-game characters.
- **Place tiles** in `assets/places/` are frames and crops from Katana ZERO: the VHS previews on the level select (`spr_vhs_preview_*`), the title slash, the bar, the video store, the cathedral window, and `spr_katanaicons`. The art belongs to Askiisoft.
- **discord-rich-presence** 0.2.5 (MIT) is compiled into `dist/kzm_discord.dll`. It is not a Discord Game SDK binary.
- **KZM** loads the mod. The platform itself is a separate project.
