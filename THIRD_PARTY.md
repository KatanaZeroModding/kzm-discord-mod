# Third-party materials

This mod does not ship Katana ZERO assets. Room names, sound ids, and object names are read from the player's own game at runtime.

- **Portraits** in `assets/avatars/` (`zero`, `fifteen`, `snow`) were made for this mod. They are not rips of Askiisoft sprites and not art from Katana SNOW.
- **discord-rich-presence** 0.2.5 (MIT) is compiled into `dist/kzm_discord.dll`. It is not a Discord Game SDK binary.
- **KZM** loads the mod. The platform itself is a separate project.
